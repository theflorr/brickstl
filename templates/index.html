brickstl

Turn a LEGO set number into printable plates.

Tests stable 90-degree orientations for every LEGO piece and chooses the orientation with the lowest estimated support and overhang risk. It also favors a low print height and broad build-plate contact. Complex pieces may still require slicer supports.
Beta: this feature is still being refined.

LDraw parts are modeled at exact injection-mould size, which is usually a touch too tight for FDM printing. This shrinks studs, pins, axle ends and clip bars by 0.15mm and grows tubes, technic holes and clip barrels by 0.15mm, so printed pieces click together instead of binding. Only round connector features are adjusted; everything else stays true to size.

The detection is heuristic and works well on standard bricks, plates and technic parts, but unusual or highly detailed geometry may be misjudged. Check your printed fit and disable this if a part looks off.
